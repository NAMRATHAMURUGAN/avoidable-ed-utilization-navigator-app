# Clinical Guidance Boundary

## Purpose

The RAG knowledge base may contain authoritative clinical or emergency-care reference material only when it has been reviewed and approved for the project.

## Current project boundary

The current knowledge base should prioritize:

- emergency-care safety boundaries
- general care navigation
- care coordination
- patient education
- Medicare service navigation
- authoritative rules and procedures

It must not become an autonomous clinical guideline engine.

## Clinical decision boundary

RAG must not:

- diagnose a patient
- prescribe treatment
- determine medical necessity
- determine whether an ED visit is avoidable
- override deterministic emergency safety rules
- use utilization-model scores as clinical probabilities

If a clinical guideline is later added, its metadata should include:

- source organization
- title
- publication/update date
- version
- effective date where applicable
- review status
- review owner
- source URL
- scope and intended audience

## Maintenance

Clinical guidance is time-sensitive. Documents should be periodically reviewed against their authoritative source before being retained for retrieval.
