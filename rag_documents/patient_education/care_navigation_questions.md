# Patient Education: Questions for Care Navigation

## Purpose

This document supports non-clinical conversations between a member and a
care navigator.

## Useful questions to discuss with a healthcare team

A member may ask: - What follow-up should I complete after this
healthcare visit? - Which clinician should I contact for follow-up? -
What symptoms or changes should prompt me to seek urgent or emergency
care? - Are there medication changes I need to understand? - What
appointments or referrals should I schedule? - What information should I
bring to my next visit? - Are there barriers to care, transportation,
scheduling, medication access, or communication that the care team can
help address?

These are prompts for discussion, not medical instructions.

## Care coordination

AHRQ describes care coordination as organizing care activities and
sharing information among participants involved in care. It emphasizes
communicating patient needs and preferences to the appropriate people.

CMS describes care-management activities that can include care
coordination, patient-centered care planning, and follow-up after
emergency-department visits and other transitions.

## Safety

The navigator should not diagnose, prescribe, determine medical
necessity, or instruct a member to avoid emergency care.
