# EMTALA Emergency-Care Rights

## Purpose

This document provides a high-level, source-backed summary of U.S. emergency-care protections under EMTALA. It is informational and does not replace legal, clinical, or hospital policy guidance.

## Core protections

CMS explains that EMTALA provides protections for people who come to a hospital emergency department that is subject to the law. These protections include:

1. An appropriate medical screening examination for an emergency medical condition.
2. Stabilizing treatment when an emergency medical condition is identified.
3. Appropriate transfer when the hospital cannot stabilize the condition within its capability and capacity.

CMS also explains that asking about insurance should not delay the medical screening examination or treatment.
