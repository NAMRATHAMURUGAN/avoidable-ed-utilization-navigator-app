# Medicare Prior Authorization and Plan Rules

## Purpose

This document provides general information about prior authorization and plan-specific rules. It is not a determination of whether a specific service requires authorization.

## Medicare Advantage

Medicare.gov explains that Medicare Advantage plans may require prior authorization for certain services or supplies.

Requirements vary by plan and service.

## Part D

Medicare.gov explains that prescription drug plans may use rules such as:

- prior authorization
- step therapy
- quantity limits
- medication safety checks

The member should check the specific plan for applicable requirements.

## RAG response boundary

Do not tell a member that a service or medication definitely requires or does not require prior authorization unless a current, authoritative, plan-specific source supports that statement.

Do not infer insurance policy from the member's utilization pattern.
