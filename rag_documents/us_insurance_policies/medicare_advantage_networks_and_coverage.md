# Medicare Advantage Networks and Coverage

## Purpose

This document summarizes general Medicare Advantage service-navigation information from Medicare.gov. It is not plan-specific benefit advice.

## General coverage

Medicare.gov states that Medicare Advantage plans must cover emergency and urgent care and almost all medically necessary services covered by Original Medicare.

Medicare Advantage plans may have different rules for how members obtain non-emergency or non-urgent services.

## Networks

For non-emergency or non-urgent care, members generally need to follow their plan's network rules. Some plan types allow out-of-network care with different cost-sharing.

Emergency and urgent care have different protections and network considerations from routine care.

## Prior authorization

A Medicare Advantage plan may require prior authorization for certain services or supplies.

A RAG response should never claim that a particular service is covered, denied, or authorized without plan-specific authoritative information.

## Safe response behavior

When a user asks a coverage question:

- identify whether the question concerns Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage when known;
- distinguish emergency/urgent care from routine services;
- avoid promising a specific cost or coverage outcome;
- direct the member to the plan or Medicare.gov for plan-specific information;
- never use utilization-model output to infer insurance coverage.
